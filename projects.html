CO2 Liquefaction Pilot Plant

Industrial research project, KAIST · 2026

Capacity
3 t/day
Plot area
~200 m²
Stage
Concept and basic engineering
My role
Process model and layout

Overview

Concept and basic engineering for a pilot facility that captures CO2 and stores it as a liquid. The process covers feed buffering, compression, pre-cooling, a cryogenic heat-exchanger train, subcooling, Joule-Thomson expansion and liquid storage, all arranged as a compact skid layout.

What I did

  • Built the process model and the heat and material balance in Aspen HYSYS.
  • Laid out the plant in an L-shaped plot of about 200 m², balancing piping runs, maintenance access and safety distances.
  • Wrote Python scripts that drive AutoCAD Plant 3D through COM to set up the drawing standard and generate the layout, so changes to the design flow through to the drawings.
  • Built a Python flow assurance tool (CoolProp) for pressure drop, liquid level and fluid property checks.
  • Built an interactive 3D plant viewer in the browser with animated flows and clickable equipment cards, used to walk the client through the design.
  • Extended the design with a liquid nitrogen cold storage module that uses ethanol as the storage medium.

Tools

  • Aspen HYSYS
  • AutoCAD Plant 3D
  • Python
  • CoolProp
  • React, Three.js

Closed-Loop Blanket Gas System for a Hot Storage Tank

Industrial research project, KAIST · Ongoing

Medium
Thermal oil, above 300 °C
Blanket gas
Nitrogen, closed loop
Duty
Many cycles per day
Stage
Concept design

Overview

A thermal energy storage tank filled with hot thermal oil charges and discharges many times a day. Every cycle moves the liquid level and pushes oil vapor into the space above it. The blanket gas system keeps that vapor space inert and at a steady low pressure without venting nitrogen to atmosphere.

The system

  • Condenser that knocks out oil vapor before the gas returns to storage.
  • Elastic gas bag that takes up the change in vapor space volume as the tank level swings.
  • Pressure control, pressure relief and vacuum relief valves, with instrumentation drawn to ISA 5.1.

Design work

  • Sizing the gas bag from the vapor space volume and the level swing over a full day of cycling.
  • Vapor pressure of the oil at operating temperature, used to set the control and relief set points.
  • Condenser duty at the peak cycling rate.

Operating figures and the client are left out because the project is under agreement.

Solar PVT and Stratified Thermal Storage Co-Simulation

Design of Energy Plants course, KAIST · 2026

Collector field
200 m² PVT
Storage tank
15 m³ stratified
Supply / return
~100 °C / ~55 °C
Sites
Daejeon and Cairo

Overview

A digital twin of a photovoltaic-thermal collector field feeding a stratified hot water tank. The same plant was run for two uses: district heating in Daejeon and absorption cooling in Cairo. Python ties together an Aspen HYSYS model of the plant and a MATLAB finite-difference model of the tank, so each tool does what it is good at.

System

  • Tank of 15 m³ (4.24 m high, 2.12 m diameter) modeled as 10 stratified layers.
  • Water and ethylene glycol (70/30) as heat transfer fluid at about 6,500 kg/h.
  • Shell-and-tube heat exchanger between the collector loop and the tank.

What I did

  • Wrote a rule-based storage controller: charge gate, state-of-charge limits, demand-aware discharge, thermocline protection and flow modulation.
  • Fixed convergence and temperature-cross problems in the HYSYS model and night-time behavior in the tank model.
  • Built a desktop dashboard that pulls live weather data and shows plant KPIs.
  • Produced the engineering package: P&ID to ISA 5.1, a HAZID worksheet, a LOPA study and an 18-page concept design report.

Tools

  • Python
  • Aspen HYSYS
  • MATLAB
  • AutoCAD Plant 3D

CSP Solar Tower Comparison: Egypt, Saudi Arabia and UAE

Independent study, KAIST · 2026

Sites
3 MENA locations
Weather data
NASA POWER
Storage model
Finite-difference tank

Overview

A Python model of a concentrated solar power tower with thermal storage, run on real weather data for sites in Egypt, Saudi Arabia and the UAE. The aim is to see how solar resource and ambient conditions change the annual yield and how hard the storage is worked.

Method

  • Hourly solar and weather data pulled from the NASA POWER API for each site.
  • Storage modeled with a finite-difference tank model to track thermocline behavior through charge and discharge.
  • Compared annual energy yield, capacity factor, LCOE sensitivity and storage use.

Early findings

  • The Saudi and UAE sites see consistently higher direct normal irradiance than the Nile Delta site, which drives deeper storage cycles.
  • The tank model picks up differences in thermocline degradation between the climates.

Tools

  • Python
  • pandas
  • matplotlib
  • MATLAB

Micro Gas Turbine (Jet Engine)

Graduation project, Ain Shams University · 2022 · Funded by the Academy of Scientific Research and Technology

Design speed
90,000 rpm
Turbine
Axial, 10 rotor / 13 stator blades
Turbine efficiency
72.5% (estimated)
Status
Designed and built

Overview

Design, analysis and manufacture of a small turbojet. The goal was a compact engine that could be built at low cost, using a turbocharger compressor and a turbine designed for it.

Design

  • Compressor: a BorgWarner turbocharger impeller, chosen for cost and surface quality. Blade angles, diameter and performance maps were checked in CF-Turbo with velocity triangles and mean-line calculations.
  • Turbine: a small axial stage designed from the mean line in CF-Turbo, with flow coefficient, loading and reaction worked out for the nozzle and rotor. Efficiency was checked against experimental data from Shapiro et al. (1957).
  • Combustor: a mathematical model for the combustion chamber in EES, and thermal expansion checks on the liner materials.

Analysis

  • Blade stress (Ansys Mechanical): at 90,000 rpm, 650 °C and 0.1 MPa on the blade face, peak von Mises stress was 349 MPa at the blade root, within limits. Tip deformation of 0.34 mm left a running clearance of about 0.43 mm.
  • Modal analysis: natural frequencies and a Campbell diagram to confirm safe operation up to 120,000 rpm.
  • CFD (Ansys Fluent, k-ω SST): pressure, velocity and temperature fields through the compressor and turbine. Compressor results matched the turbocharger maps, and turbine efficiency came out at about 72.5%.
  • Cycle model (MATLAB): Brayton cycle for specific thrust, thermal efficiency and fuel use across ambient conditions and altitudes.

Tools

  • Ansys Fluent
  • Ansys Mechanical
  • CF-Turbo
  • SolidWorks
  • MATLAB
  • EES

Hybrid Electric Vehicle Model and Control Strategy

Ain Shams University research center

Architecture
P2 parallel hybrid
Drive cycle
FTP-75
Platform
MATLAB / Simulink

Overview

A working Simulink model of a P2 parallel hybrid, built step by step: first a battery electric vehicle, then a conventional vehicle, then the hybrid that reuses parts and control logic from both. That kept the three models consistent and made the hybrid easier to debug.

Model

  • FTP-75 urban drive cycle (2,474 s) as the speed demand.
  • Environment block for temperature, altitude, wind and road conditions.
  • Longitudinal driver model for throttle and braking.
  • Vehicle controller for speed and torque split, and the passenger car powertrain with engine and electric motor.
  • Plots of battery current, state of charge, fuel economy, speed and torque to judge the strategy.
