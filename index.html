Ahmed Mahgoub

Graduate Researcher, IESEL Lab, KAIST

I work as a graduate researcher at the Innovative Energy Systems Engineering Lab (IESEL) at KAIST in Daejeon, South Korea, while completing my M.S. There I work on thermal energy storage, cryogenic process design and solar thermal systems for industrial partners, from process simulation and plant layout to P&IDs and safety reviews.

The direction I am building toward is machine learning and AI for sustainability: using data-driven models to design and run cleaner energy systems. Before KAIST I worked in industry as an R&D engineer on power transformers at Elsewedy Electric and on AC refrigeration systems at Carrier Midea.
